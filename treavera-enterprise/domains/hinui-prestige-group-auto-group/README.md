# Hinui Prestige Group Auto Group

Private family-asset acquisition and automotive operating domain within Treavera Enterprise.

## Purpose

Hinui Prestige Group Auto Group is designed as a private structure for family-controlled acquisition, custody, utilization, lifecycle management, and disposition of automotive and related high-value assets.

The architecture separates:

- family governance and authorization
- asset acquisition and funding
- dealership/automotive operations
- custody and ownership records
- vehicle utilization and allocation
- insurance, maintenance, warranty and compliance records
- resale, transfer and estate workflows
- financial and treasury interfaces

## Design principles

1. **Private by default** — operational data and family information are segregated from public-facing dealer activity.
2. **Entity-aware** — legal ownership, beneficial interests, custody and user allocation are represented separately.
3. **Policy-controlled** — acquisition and disposition require explicit authorization according to family governance policies.
4. **Auditable** — every material decision produces evidence and a traceable record.
5. **No commingling** — family assets, operating-company assets and third-party/customer assets remain distinct in the system model.
6. **Lifecycle-based** — each vehicle has a controlled lifecycle from sourcing through acquisition, custody, utilization, maintenance, valuation and exit.

## Important boundary

This repository is an operating-system and governance design. It does not itself establish a legal entity, trust, tax treatment, dealer licence, financing arrangement, or ownership structure. Those require appropriate professional and regulatory advice in the applicable jurisdiction.
