# RWA / Digital Provenance Boundary

The HPG demo may represent an asset digitally for provenance, workflow and family reporting.

It must not imply that a digital record itself transfers legal title.

## Allowed in sandbox

- asset identity
- provenance chain
- acquisition evidence
- ownership-structure references
- valuation snapshots
- entitlement metadata as a non-legal demo concept
- simulated token identifiers clearly labelled `DEMO`

## Disabled by default

- security-token issuance
- fractional investment offers
- public token sales
- secondary trading
- custody of digital assets
- stablecoin/payment settlement
- exchange functionality
- yield promises
- personalised regulated investment advice

## Design principle

If a future RWA implementation creates a financial product, financial advice service, managed investment product, derivative, custody service or other regulated activity, the system must stop at the compliance boundary until the required legal structure, registrations, licences, disclosures, controls and service providers are established.
